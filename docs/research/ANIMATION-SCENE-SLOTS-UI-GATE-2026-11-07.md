the whole inventory and completed edits/history/resource publication compare its
complete authored shape. Slot growth increases those allocation/serialization
costs and live per-slot render/projection work even when keys/tracks stay under
their unchanged per-clip limits. No full project IPC is added per pointer/frame;
native image loading remains guarded cached data URL requests, not a new server.

SceneTree, draw-order rows, owner/end options and synthetic timeline rows are
ordinary nonvirtualized DOM/SVG lists. Tree projection scans slots per bone;
selection/owner lookups scan or map the actual inventory. If512selected slots are

slot count. Selection narrows the visible context but cannot be used to hide the
unselected/all-context cost. Clip interval masks and inactive selection overlays
also depend on the actual slots/geometry; repeated PNGs do not erase those costs.
